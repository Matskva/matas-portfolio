Paintings by Matas, London fine artist

Matas is a fine artist and painter based in London, working in oil and acrylic on canvas and on banknotes. These original expressionist paintings by Matas circle identity, corruption, money and the slow erosion of the self. Matas is available for commissions and gallery enquiries in London and internationally.
