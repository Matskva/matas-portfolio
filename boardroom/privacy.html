Boardroom FCPrivacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Boardroom FC is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the game only which item was bought so it can be added to your club.

Your Saved Game

Your club, settings, daily reward streak and progress are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud device backups, Apple may include the saved game in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your scores (trophies won, seasons completed and club bank balance) and the achievements you unlock to Game Center, so they appear on leaderboards with your Game Center name. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; we don't receive your Game Center details. You can sign out of Game Center, or limit who sees your profile, in your device's Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders about daily rewards, exclusive signings and matches are scheduled on your device. To send them at a time that suits you, the game remembers the hour of day of your recent visits, on your device only. No information is sent to a server to do this, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the game and at https://mat4s.com/boardroom/privacy.html.
