Boardroom FCSupport

Boardroom FC is a single-player football club management game for iPhone. You're the chairman: hire the manager, sign the players, grow the stadium and keep the board happy.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email matas@mail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Purchases

Energy refills and cash packs are consumable: they apply to the club you're playing straight away and can't be restored on another device. If a purchase didn't arrive, close and reopen the game while connected to the internet. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your saved game

Your club is saved only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes the save. We can't recover a lost save because we never receive it.

Notifications

Matchday reminders can be turned on or off in the game's settings, or in the iPhone's Settings app under Boardroom FC.
