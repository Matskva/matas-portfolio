ArgentSupport

Argent is a manual camera for iPhone: RAW and ProRAW photos with full control over focus, ISO, shutter and white balance, and cinema-style video with shutter angle, false color, ProRes and HDR.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email matas@mail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Free trial and unlock

Argent is free to download with a 7-day free trial. After that, a single in-app purchase unlocks it for good. There is no subscription, and nothing is charged when the trial ends.

The trial and the unlock belong to your Apple Account, so they carry over to a new iPhone or a reinstall. If Argent asks you to unlock it again, tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Formats

HEIC uses Apple's processing. PURE is a single RAW frame developed neutrally, with no computational processing. RAW saves the sensor's Bayer DNG. ProRAW saves Apple ProRAW on iPhones that support it. Some formats and video options depend on the lens and iPhone model; Argent only offers what your iPhone can record.

Your photos and videos

Everything you shoot is kept in Argent and, if you allow it, saved to your Photos library. Deleting the app deletes the copies kept in Argent, not the ones in Photos.

Permissions

Argent needs the camera to work, the microphone to record sound with video, and permission to add to Photos to save there. You can change these at any time in the iPhone's Settings app under Argent.
