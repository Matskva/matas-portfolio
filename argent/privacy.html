ArgentPrivacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Argent is a camera app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. Argent has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your photos, videos or settings anywhere.

Camera and microphone

Argent uses the camera to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos, and the microphone to record sound with videos. Images and sound are processed on your iPhone only.

Photos

Argent asks for permission to add to your Photos library so it can save what you shoot there. It can only add new items; it cannot read or change the rest of your library.

Location

Argent does not access your location, and it does not add location to your photos or videos.

Data on your device

Your captures and settings (such as the last format and video settings you used) are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, except for anything already saved to Photos. If you use iCloud backups or iCloud Photos, Apple may store copies under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple tells the app only which items your Apple Account owns.

Children

Because Argent collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at https://mat4s.com/argent/privacy.html.
